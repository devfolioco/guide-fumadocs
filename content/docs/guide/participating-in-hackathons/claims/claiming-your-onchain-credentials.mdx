---
title: Claiming your Onchain Credentials
description: Step-by-step guide to claim your Soulbound Token (SBT) on Devfolio.
---

<Callout type="info" title="Know more about Onchain Credentials">
Know more about onchain credentials by [clicking here](/docs/guide/onchain-credentials).
</Callout>

<Steps>
<Step>
**Go to My Claims**

Click on your profile in the top-right corner and choose 'My Claims' or click [here](https://devfolio.co/claims).

<img src="/assets/image 135 (1) (2).webp" alt="Navigate to My Claims" />
</Step>
<Step>
**Select the Onchain Credentials card**

<img src="/assets/image 133.webp" alt="Onchain credentials card" />
</Step>
<Step>
**Choose the credential you want to claim**

<img src="/assets/image 137 (3) (1) (1).webp" alt="Select credential" />
</Step>
<Step>
**Link your Ethereum wallet to Devfolio**

<img src="/assets/image 139.webp" alt="Link Ethereum wallet" />
</Step>
<Step>
**Continue with Ethereum**

Click on **Continue with Ethereum** and finish the transaction.

<img src="/assets/image 146 (1).webp" alt="Confirm transaction" />
</Step>
<Step>
**Confirm Wallet**

Double-check the wallet details and click on **Confirm Wallet**. For `gasless` Onchain Credentials, the transaction is executed by Devfolio.

<img src="/assets/image (40).webp" alt="Confirm wallet" />
</Step>
</Steps>

🎉 **Hooray!** The SBT will be sent to your connected wallet. You can view it on OpenSea & EAS Scan and tweet about it!

<img src="/assets/image (41).webp" alt="SBT claimed success screen" />
