---
title: Claiming Your Bounties
description: Step-by-step guide to claiming bounties after a Devfolio hackathon.
---

Follow these steps to claim the bounty you’ve won through a hackathon initiative.

<Steps>
<Step>
**Go to My Claims**

Click on your profile in the top-right corner and select **"My Claims."**

<img src="/assets/image 135 (1).webp" alt="My Claims" />
</Step>
<Step>
**Select the Bounties Card**

<img src="/assets/pika-1682066146228-1x 2.webp" alt="Bounties Card" />
</Step>
<Step>
**Click "Claim Bounties" for your bounty initiative**

<img src="/assets/pika-1682066768151-1x 1.webp" alt="Claim bounties" />
</Step>
<Step>
**Start the KYC process**

<img src="/assets/Screenshot (3236).webp" alt="Start KYC" />
</Step>
<Step>
**Verify Your Identity**

<img src="/assets/Screenshot (3237).webp" alt="Verify identity" />
</Step>
<Step>
**Select your Document's Country and Type**

<img src="/assets/Screenshot (3239).webp" alt="Select document type" />
</Step>
<Step>
**Submit Documents and Await Confirmation**

<img src="/assets/Screenshot (3245).webp" alt="Submit KYC" />
</Step>
<Step>
**Click Confirm Wallet to complete payout**

<img src="/assets/pika-1682066851084-1x 1 (1).webp" alt="Confirm Wallet" />
</Step>
</Steps>

**All done!** Sit back and relax. Your bounties will be sent to your wallet soon 🎉

<img src="/assets/pika-1682073331112-1x 1.webp" alt="Success!" />
