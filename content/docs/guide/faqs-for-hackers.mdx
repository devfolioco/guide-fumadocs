<Accordions type="single">

<Accordion title="Why are my team members unable to initiate submissions on Devfolio?">
If you are participating as a team, submissions can only be made by the **team admin**.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I update a project after I have submitted it for judging?">
Yes! You can update your project at [devfolio.co/projects](https://devfolio.co/projects). Any updates made will show up in the project history.

<Cards>
  <Card title="Projects Tab" href="/docs/guide/your-devfolio-profile/projects" />
</Cards>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="My friend cannot join my team. What could be the possible reason?">
**General**

- **The team code entered is invalid** — Double-check the code and re-enter.
- **Your friend is already in a team** — They’ll need to leave their current team to join yours.

**Before Submitting the Team for Review**

- **Your friend applied individually** — They can withdraw their individual application and join the team.

**After Submitting the Team for Review**

- **Your friend applied individually** — You can only edit teammates after your application has been accepted. Only **accepted hackers** can join your accepted team.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I apply individually in a strictly team hackathon?">
Yes, you can apply and even get accepted individually. However, to submit a project, joining or creating a team is **mandatory**.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why am I not able to submit my profile for review?">
Make sure your application is **100% complete**. Just finish all required questions and you’ll be ready to go!
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where can I see submissions made by other hackers?">
The submissions are always visible on the `Submissions` tab under any particular hackathon. Click on the specific submission card to explore the project.

<Cards>
  <Card title="View Projects" href="/docs/guide/Projects/view-submissions" />
</Cards>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where can I see the winners?">
After the hackathon ends, the organizing team can highlight the winners under the `Projects` tab. The winners of the hackathon or any other category will get a special badge on their project card.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How can I submit feedback to organizers?">
When the hackathon ends, check your dashboard and there you will find a custom card to share your feedback anonymously!
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I delete a project uploaded on Devfolio?">
- You **cannot delete** a project submitted to a hackathon.
- For side-projects on [devfolio.co/projects](https://devfolio.co/projects), email [help@devfolio.co](mailto:help@devfolio.co) with your reason.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What if someone withdraws by mistake and wants to rejoin the hackathon?">
Drop us a mail at [hello@devfolio.co](mailto:hello@devfolio.co) — we’ll help you out!
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can we update a team after submitting it for review?">
Yes, you are allowed to update the team before making the submission to the hackathon. Once you make the submission, you won't be able to make any further changes.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What is the deadline for joining/creating a team at a hackathon?">
You can join/create a team before the submission deadline. Upon making the submission, your team will be finalized and you won't be able to make any changes to it.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I delete my Devfolio account?">
You can delete your account from the Settings Tab while logged in to your Devfolio account. Refer to the `Settings Tab` for more details.

<Cards>
  <Card title="Settings Tab" href="/docs/guide/your-devfolio-profile/settings" />
</Cards>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I stop receiving email announcements from a particular hackathon?">
1. Visit [https://devfolio.co/hackathons/applied](https://devfolio.co/hackathons/applied)
2. Click on the hackathon to open the dashboard
3. In the **Announcements** section, turn off **Email Notifications**
