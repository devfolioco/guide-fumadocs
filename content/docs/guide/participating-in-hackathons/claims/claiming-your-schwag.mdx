---
title: Claiming your Schwag
description: Step-by-step walkthrough to claim your Devfolio schwag after participating in a hackathon.
---

<Steps>
<Step>
**Go to My Claims**

Click on your profile in the top-right corner and choose **"My Claims"** or directly visit [devfolio.co/claims](https://devfolio.co/claims).

<img src="/assets/image 135 (1) (1).webp" alt="My Claims" />
</Step>
<Step>
**Select the Schwag Card**

<img src="/assets/image 133 (1).webp" alt="Schwag category card" />
</Step>
<Step>
**Click Claim Schwag**

Find the eligible schwag and click **Claim schwag**.

<img src="/assets/pika-1682066192886-1x 1.webp" alt="Claim schwag button" />
</Step>
<Step>
**Fill Out the Shipping Form**

Enter the required details and submit the form.

<img src="/assets/pika-1682066306949-1x 1.webp" alt="Shipping form" />
</Step>
</Steps>

**Done!** That’s it — your Devfolio goodies are on the way 🚚

<img src="/assets/image (39).webp" alt="Confirmation screen" />
