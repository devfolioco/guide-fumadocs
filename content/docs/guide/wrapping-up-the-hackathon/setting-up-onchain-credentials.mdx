## Step-by-Step Guide

### 1. Go to Organizer Dashboard

Click on your profile in the top-right corner and select <code>Organizer Dashboard</code>.

<img src="/assets/image (183).webp" alt="Access Organizer Dashboard" />

<Caption>Access your Organizer Dashboard</Caption>



### 2. Enable Onchain Credentials in Brand Tab

Navigate to the <code>Brand</code> tab of your hackathon and enable Onchain Credentials.

<img src="/assets/image (184).webp" alt="Enable Onchain Credentials" />

<Caption>Enable Onchain Credentials toggle</Caption>



### 3. Fill in Collection Details

Complete the collection information and select the intended receiver.

<img src="/assets/image (185).webp" alt="Enter credential details" />

<Caption>Set collection name and recipient</Caption>



### 4. Customize and Upload

Preview the credential template or adjust its colors, then click <code>Upload</code>.

<img src="/assets/image (186).webp" alt="Upload Onchain Credential design" />

<Caption>Customize and upload your credential</Caption>



### 5. Add Custom Onchain Credentials

For volunteers or guests not listed in the dashboard, you can issue custom credentials.

<img src="/assets/image (187).webp" alt="Add custom Onchain credentials" />

<Caption>Manually add credentials for guests or volunteers</Caption>



## Enabling Claims for Participants

Access the <strong>Onchain Creds</strong> tab in your Organizer dashboard to begin issuing credentials.



### 1. Finalize Claims

By default, everyone listed is eligible. Toggle off eligibility for those who shouldn't claim.

<img src="/assets/image (188).webp" alt="Finalize claim eligibility" />

<Caption>Toggle eligibility for each participant</Caption>



### 2. Track Claim Status

After finalization, you can monitor who has claimed their credentials.

<img src="/assets/image (189).webp" alt="Track Onchain claim status" />

<Caption>Track whether credentials were claimed</Caption>



### 3. Add More People (Volunteers, Guests)

Click <code>+ Add people</code> to include others. Ensure you’ve created credentials for them via the Brand tab.

<img src="/assets/image (190).webp" alt="Add additional recipients" />

<Caption>Add extra people like volunteers</Caption>

<img src="/assets/image (191).webp" alt="Confirm custom addition" />

<Caption>Make sure they have a credential ready</Caption>
