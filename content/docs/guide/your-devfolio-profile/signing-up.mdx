### Sign in with Google (Magic Link)

<Video src="https://www.loom.com/embed/fbf9a7281c04472ca3b97e27642bc6d0" title="Sign in with Google" />

You can hit the button **"Sign in with magic link"** to sign in.  
This magic link is valid for **30 minutes**.  
If it expires, no worries — just request a new one!


### Link Multiple Accounts

You can link multiple accounts on the platform via **Account Settings**.  
This enables you to sign in and manage all your linked accounts effortlessly.

Connect your accounts here:  
[https://devfolio.co/settings/](https://devfolio.co/settings/)
